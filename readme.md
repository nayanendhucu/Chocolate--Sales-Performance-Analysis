🍫 Chocolate Sales Performance Analysis

Power BI | DAX | Business Analytics

📌 Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world business scenario in which I take the role of a Business Analyst at a chocolate manufacturing company.

Management requires a quick overview of overall sales performance, employee productivity, sales target achievement and salesperson rankings.

The objective is to translate business requirements into meaningful DAX measures using Microsoft Power BI and provide insights that support management reporting and decision-making.

Business Scenario: Simulated Ferrero-inspired chocolate sales business case.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall chocolate sales performance.
- Calculate shipment-related KPIs.
- Evaluate individual salesperson performance.
- Analyse employee-specific product category sales.
- Calculate combined sales for a selected employee group.
- Define and monitor individual sales targets.
- Classify sales performance using conditional logic.
- Rank sales executives and identify the Top 3 performers.

💼 Business Requirements & Tasks

1. Overall Sales Performance

- Calculate Total Boxes.
- Calculate Boxes Per Shipment.
- Analyse Boxes Per Shipment at employee level.
- Calculate Amount Per Shipment.

2. Individual Employee Analysis

- Calculate Beverly Moffett's total sales.
- Compare individual sales with overall sales.
- Analyse Beverly's sales specifically for the Bars product category.

3. Group Sales Analysis

- Calculate combined sales generated by Group 1.
- Apply multiple employee conditions using logical operators.

4. Sales Target Achievement

- Define a sales target of ₹20,00,000 per salesperson.
- Compare actual sales against the target.
- Classify employees as Target Achieved or Target Failed.

5. Sales Performance Classification

Using SWITCH, classify sales performance into:

Category| Sales Amount
Low| Below ₹10,00,000
Medium| ₹10,00,000–₹20,00,000
High| Above ₹20,00,000

6. Employee Ranking

- Rank employees based on total sales using RANKX.
- Apply descending order and DENSE ranking.
- Identify the Top 3 sales executives.
- Classify remaining employees as Others.

📚 DAX Concepts Applied

Function| Application
SUM| Sales and box aggregation
DISTINCTCOUNT| Unique shipment counting
DIVIDE| Average and ratio calculations
CALCULATE| Filter context modification
FILTER| Conditional filtering
ALL| Removing existing filters
IF| Target achievement
SWITCH| Performance classification
RANKX| Employee ranking
Logical OR ( || )| Multiple conditions
Logical AND ( && )| Combined conditions
