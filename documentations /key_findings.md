6. Key Business Insights

1. Employee performance: Barr Faughny, Ches Bonnell and Brien Boise are the top three sales performers.
2. Target achievement: 10 out of 25 employees achieved the ₹20 lakh sales target.
3. Performance distribution: 11 employees fall into the Medium category, while 4 are classified as Low.
4. Employee development: There is an opportunity to improve the performance of employees who have not achieved their targets.
5. Individual contribution: Beverie Moffett generated ₹11,51,977.50 in sales.

7. Business Recommendations

7.1 Recognise and Reward High Performers

Introduce performance-based incentives, recognition and rewards for employees who consistently achieve or exceed their sales targets.

7.2 Encourage Knowledge Sharing

Organise knowledge-sharing sessions where the top three performers present their sales strategies, customer-handling techniques and approaches to achieving targets.

Other employees can learn from these practices and apply relevant strategies to improve their own performance.

7.3 Improve Low-Performing Employee Performance

Provide targeted sales training, individual guidance and regular performance reviews for employees classified under the Low category.

7.4 Evaluate Product Category Performance

Compare sales across product categories, including Bars and other categories, to identify products with stronger sales demand.

Use these insights to guide marketing campaigns, inventory planning and future investment decisions.

Sales performance alone does not establish product profitability. Cost and profit data would be required to make investment decisions based on profitability.

8. Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how Power BI and DAX can support sales performance evaluation, employee ranking, target monitoring and business decision-making.

The findings can help management identify high-performing employees, understand performance gaps and develop strategies to improve overall sales performance.
