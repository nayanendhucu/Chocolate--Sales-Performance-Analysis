Business Requirements Document

1. Business Scenario

Imagine working as a Business Analyst for a chocolate manufacturing and sales company.

Management wants to understand the company's sales performance, evaluate individual salesperson contributions, compare employee performance against sales targets, and identify the highest-performing employees.

This project uses a simulated chocolate sales dataset to address these business requirements using Power BI and DAX.

2. Business Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

- Evaluate overall sales performance.
- Understand the total number of boxes sold and shipments processed.
- Analyse average boxes and sales amount per shipment.
- Measure individual salesperson performance.
- Analyse selected employees' sales contributions.
- Compare group-level sales performance.
- Evaluate employee achievement against sales targets.
- Classify sales performance into Low, Medium and High categories.
- Identify and rank the top-performing employees.

4. Sales Target Classification

The sales target is ₹20,00,000 per salesperson.

Performance Category| Condition
Low| Sales below ₹10,00,000
Medium| Sales from ₹10,00,000 to ₹20,00,000
High| Sales above ₹20,00,000

5. Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis is designed to help management:

- Understand overall sales performance.
- Identify employees contributing to revenue.
- Monitor target achievement.
- Compare employee groups.
- Recognise high-performing employees.
- Make more informed business decisions using sales data.
